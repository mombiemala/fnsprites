How Fortnite Sprites work (the quick version)

Sprites are collectible companions you find and equip in a match for a special Sprite Power — a passive or active ability like a heal on damage, a burst of mobility, or better loot. You don’t keep one just by catching it: you have to extract it before the match ends, and if you’re eliminated first it’s lost. Keep a Sprite equipped and it levels from 1 to 5; extract it at Level 5 and it’s mastered, which unlocks rewards toward the rest of your collection. Every Sprite also comes in finishes — Normal, Gold, Cheat Master, Loot Hacker, Bounty Hunter and the seasonal Trick-or-Treat, plus the older Gummy, Galaxy, Gem, Holofoil, Cube and Quack — each a different look that layers a small bonus on top of the base ability. This tracker records which Sprites and finishes you’ve collected and mastered, and the guides below explain each system in depth.
